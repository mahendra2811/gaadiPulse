# GaadiPulse social launch plan

## Purpose

Introduce GaadiPulse to vehicle owners, small fleet operators, family businesses and drivers in a way that starts with a recognizable problem, explains the practical solution, and invites people to try the Android app. Use this as the source document for LinkedIn and X posts, campaign visuals and future feature-specific posts.

**App:** GaadiPulse  
**Tagline:** Every Vehicle. Every Detail.  
**Google Play:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pooniya.gaadipulse  
**Product overview:** https://www.pooniya.com/portfolio/gaadipulse

## Product story

### The problem

Vehicle information often lives in different places: a driver's messages, paper receipts, fuel notes, an owner's memory and separate spreadsheets. When an owner is not with the vehicle every day, it can be difficult to see what has been spent on each vehicle and keep its fuel and expense history together. For a business with several vehicles or machines, the picture gets harder to maintain. Drivers also need a simple way to record what they know without being given access to every vehicle or every record.

Keep the problem human and familiar. Do not imply that every driver is dishonest, that owners can watch a vehicle live, or that record-keeping alone prevents fraud.

### The solution

GaadiPulse is a vehicle logbook for recording fuel and other expenses, organizing information vehicle by vehicle, and sharing access with the people who need it. Owners can share a vehicle with drivers or other collaborators and manage access for that vehicle. The app is designed for a wide range of vehicles, from two-wheelers and cars to commercial vehicles and heavy machinery. Depending on the vehicle, its records can include distance or engine-hour readings.

### Core message

**Know what is being recorded for each vehicle, keep fuel and expenses organized, and share the right vehicle access with your team.**

### Message pillars

1. **Visibility:** Keep a vehicle's fuel and expense records together so an owner can review the information recorded for it.
2. **Shared responsibility:** A vehicle can be shared with drivers and other collaborators with per-vehicle roles and permissions.
3. **Built for mixed fleets:** Use the same logbook idea across bikes, cars, vans, trucks, tractors and machinery.
4. **Practical records:** Record fuel and non-fuel expenses; readings can be relevant as KM or engine hours, depending on the vehicle.
5. **Accessible start:** The product reference describes GaadiPulse as free, with sign-in. Say “free to use” only while that is true for the live app and avoid “free forever” unless explicitly guaranteed.

## Accuracy and claim guardrails

Use claims grounded in the app and its current public listing. Before publishing, confirm the current app build still supports every feature named in the post.

- Say **“record and review fuel and expenses”**, not “automatically tracks every rupee” or “detects misuse.”
- Say **“share access with drivers/collaborators”**, not “track your driver” or “see their live location.” GPS tracking is not part of the product scope.
- Say **“view the information recorded for a vehicle”**, not “real-time vehicle monitoring.”
- Say **“supports vehicles from bikes to heavy machinery”** only if the current app’s vehicle setup and records support the examples shown.
- Use **“free to use”** only if current Google Play availability and in-app terms support it. Do not say “free forever.”
- Avoid promising perfect mileage, automatic fuel detection, fraud prevention, maintenance prediction, document uploads, or features that are deferred.
- Do not show real customer names, registration numbers, phone numbers, receipts, account email addresses or financial data in screenshots.
- Clearly label mock data in designed visuals; use the actual app UI for product screenshots wherever possible.

## Platform adaptation notes

### LinkedIn

- Use a first-person builder voice if posting from the maker’s profile.
- Keep the first two lines specific and recognizable; put the app name after the problem.
- Use short paragraphs and one clear question to invite useful feedback.
- Use 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end. Put the Play link in the body for direct installation.
- Prefer a real privacy-safe screenshot or founder photo plus product visual over a poster crowded with copy.

### X

- Put the problem and product in the first post; keep one link and one point per post.
- Use a short standalone launch post, then a thread only if showing a real workflow (problem, how records are organized, sharing access, supported vehicles, link).
- Keep hashtags to zero, one or two. Mention the app’s limitation honestly if a reply asks about GPS, automation or unsupported features.
- For an image post, make the graphic understandable without reading a long thread.

## Engagement and reuse

- Ask a real question: “How do you currently keep fuel and repair records for your vehicles?”
- Invite owners and drivers to describe the most useful record they wish they had at month end.
- Respond to feature requests as feedback, not promises. Note them for product planning before committing publicly.
- Reuse questions and feedback for future posts only with permission and without exposing private business or vehicle information.
- After publishing, track meaningful indicators: link clicks, Play listing visits if available, installs, comments from target users and direct feedback. Do not call the campaign successful based only on impressions.

## Pre-publish checklist

- Confirm app availability and the Play link open correctly for the intended audience.
- Recheck every named feature in the current published build and listing.
- Confirm whether the app is currently free to use before making a pricing claim.
- Check app screenshots for demo data, unreadable UI, personal details and vehicle registration numbers.
- Ensure the image says shared records/access rather than live tracking or guaranteed cost savings.
- Check spelling: **GaadiPulse**, not “Gaadi Pulse”; use the exact tagline **Every Vehicle. Every Detail.**
- Add alt text on LinkedIn where available: “GaadiPulse vehicle logbook concept showing an owner, driver, phone and several types of vehicles.”
- Preview the square and 4:5 crops on mobile and confirm the CTA and subjects remain visible.
- Use the Play Store and portfolio links as plain clickable URLs in the caption.
